Banana Pudding

Description

Featuring almond milk, vanilla protein powder, and vanilla yogurt, this banana pudding is the perfect better-for-you dessert that tastes great AND will help you reach your protein goals. The protein is an added boost, but trust me—this still tastes every bit like the classic banana pudding you know and love.

I roughly calculated that each serving of this banana pudding should provide about 4 times as much protein as a regular store-bought version. Grab this banana pudding as a snack before the gym, a sweet breakfast treat, or a late-night dessert. It’s slightly less sweet than regular banana pudding too, which makes it the perfect adult version of a childhood classic.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup vanilla protein powder (25 g protein)
  • 1 (3.4-oz.) pkg. vanilla instant Jell-O pudding
  • 1 1/2 cups cold whole or plant-based milk
  • 1 1/4 cups heavy cream
  • 1/3 cup high-protein sweetened vanilla yogurt (such as Siggi’s)
  • 3 ripe bananas, thinly sliced
  • 27 Nilla Wafers, lightly crumbled, plus 4 whole Wafers

Instructions

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk protein powder and Jell-O. While whisking, slowly pour in milk until no lumps remain, about 1 minute. Cover bowl in plastic wrap and refrigerate at least 40 minutes and up to overnight. The consistency will be slightly looser than regular pudding at this point.

  2. In a large bowl, using a handheld mixer on medium-high speed, beat cream until soft peaks form. Add yogurt and beat until stiff peaks form, 30 to 60 seconds more. Scoop out 1/2 cup whipped cream mixture and transfer to an airtight container. Refrigerate until ready to use.

  3. Using a rubber spatula, combine remaining whipped cream mixture with milk mixture until no streaks remain.

  4. In a small jar or serving glass, layer 1/4 cup yogurt mixture, 4 to 5 banana slices, and about 2 crumbled Nilla Wafers. Repeat layers one more time. Finish with 1/4 cup yogurt mixture. Repeat with remaining jars or glasses. Refrigerate at least 3 hours, and covered, up to overnight.

  5. Top each with a spoonful of reserved whipped cream, a whole Nilla Wafer, and more banana slices.
